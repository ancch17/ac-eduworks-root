How the qualification stacks up

      Where the dates came from. Every date here was read off the SMU Academy module pages. Three modules — A Practical Approach to Generative AI, Prompt Engineering, and Responsible Use and Ethical Management — load their schedule tables through a script that does not render to a plain fetch, so only the next intake date is confirmed for those. They are marked start date only and their second day is not shown. Check those three on the SMU site before you commit to anything.
